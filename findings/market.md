## Findings
- [M-001] Snowflake Marketplace supports paid listings with flat-fee or usage-based pricing, per-consumer offers and 1-90 day free trials, but paid listings are limited to specific provider and consumer regions. | https://docs.snowflake.com/en/collaboration/collaboration-listings-about | high | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-002] Snowflake-style marketplaces sell dataset or share access, not curated answers to a buyer query with output controls and seller-side release review; this is a gap vs the idea (inference from listing model). | https://docs.snowflake.com/en/collaboration/collaboration-listings-about | low | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-003] A vendor comparison describes Databricks Marketplace pricing as largely contract-based and AWS Data Exchange as subscription entitlements with hard-to-model cost and gaps in niche providers; single competitor-written source. | https://www.airframe.ai/product/aws-data-exchange/compare | low | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-004] AWS Clean Rooms meters compute in CRPU-hours with per-second billing and minimums (example $4.00 per CRPU-hour for PySpark, 10 min minimum), and the collaboration can set which member pays; this is a metered pay-per-query-like precedent. | https://aws.amazon.com/clean-rooms/pricing/ | med | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-005] LiveRamp Clean Room lets data owners restrict which queries run, filter sensitive columns and revoke access; its templates target advertising use cases (overlap, attribution, incrementality), so it is not built for unstructured R&D knowledge or agent buyers. | https://docs.liveramp.com/connect/en/liveramp-clean-room-privacy-and-governance.html | med | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-006] Expert networks sell human time at roughly 800-1,450 USD per hour or call (competitor and secondary sources, conflicting), with prepaid or credit models and subscription transcript libraries; Third Bridge claims 100,000+ transcripts. Shows corporate buyers pay for expert insight, but delivery is human and not agent-queryable. | https://allmind.ai/research/third-bridge-alternatives-expert-insights | low | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-007] MELLODDY: ten pharma companies trained shared models on 2.6 billion+ confidential data points without moving data, and all partners saw gains. Evidence that pharma will share via privacy-preserving infrastructure, but under a consortium with joint governance and not pay-per-query sales. | https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11005050/ | med | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-008] AGAINST (incumbent): Apheris coordinates a consortium (BMS, Takeda, AbbVie, J&J, Astex) contributing proprietary data to train OpenFold3 via federated setup; consortium models already occupy the pharma data-collaboration niche. | https://www.theinsightpartners.com/news/big-pharma-teams-up-to-train-ai-for-drug-research | low | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-009] Coalition Against Major Diseases pooled data from 11 failed Alzheimer's-type trials (about 4,000 patients) from five drugmakers into a database run by Critical Path Institute, open to qualified outsiders. Precedent that failed-trial data is shared, but free via a nonprofit, which weakens willingness to pay. | https://alzforum.org/node/37481 | low | US | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-010] AGAINST (sellers): commentary says companies prefer to publicise successes over failures and fear vulnerability from disclosing failure; a former Merck executive proposed only pre-phase-II sharing with no IP claimed, implying later-stage findings stay proprietary. | https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/article/why-sharing-failures-can-speed-up-innovation | low | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-011] Demand signal for failed-experiment knowledge: IBM Research reports a chemistry language model tuned on successes plus failures outperformed one tuned on successes only, and a Münster result found removing negative results hurt yield prediction. | https://research.ibm.com/blog/how-to-make-ai-models-more-accurate-embrace-failure | med | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-012] Materials-science perspective (2026) argues databases omit failed syntheses so AI models are overconfident, and failures outnumber successes near ten to one; supports chemistry and materials as early buyer verticals. | https://www.newswise.com/articles/from-dark-data-to-smart-labs-a-roadmap-for-reliable-ai-in-materials-science | med | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-013] Gap: no source found showing anyone paying for negative or unused-experiment datasets, and a drug-discovery blog says a bare did-not-work label is of little use without method provenance and structured context, which raises seller cost to prepare knowledge. | https://bayespharma.onrender.com/blog/negative-data-moat-failed-experiments-drug-discovery | low | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-014] Seller blockers: a literature review found 21 data-monetization challenges in established firms across legal and regulatory, security and privacy, business model and organizational categories. | https://research.aalto.fi/en/publications/data-monetization-challenges-in-established-organizations-a-syste/ | med | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-015] In the 451 Research 2022 survey, about 30% of firms that value data monetization cited legal or compliance requirements and just under a third cited external data-exchange requirements as barriers; measures barriers to being data-driven, not refusal to sell. | https://spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/research/data-monetization-on-the-rise-driven-by-the-most-digitally-mature-enterprises | med | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-016] Mayer Brown notes traditional IP gives limited protection for data, making contracts with third parties the central control; supports legal-review cost and IP fear as seller blockers. | https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2022/01/addressing-the-ip-pitfalls-in-data-monetisation-projects | med | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-017] Cloudflare Pay Per Crawl uses HTTP 402 for per-request charges to AI crawlers, still reported as private beta in 2026 with no public price levels; precedent for per-request machine payment, adoption unproven. | https://ppc.land/cloudflare-launches-pay-per-crawl-to-monetize-ai-content-access/ | low | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-018] India: an IndiaAI working group recommended the India Dataset Platform let providers set one-time, subscription or licence pricing including for non-personal data, and a national data exchange is envisioned; a public-sector signal for data monetisation but no company sellers named and no per-query model. | https://inc42.com/buzz/indiaai-working-group-monetisation-non-personal-data | med | India | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-019] India: an EPAM article (2026) argues Indian biopharma lacks AI-ready data because it is fragmented; implies both a need and a high seller preparation cost (headline only, not fetched). | https://www.epam.com/about/newsroom/in-the-news/2026/fragmented-pharmaceuticals-why-indian-biopharma-does-not-have-ai-ready-data | low | India | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-020] India: a 2026 BioSpectrum op-ed urges Indian pharma to collaborate and share assets to de-risk drug discovery; advocacy and not evidence of actual behaviour. | https://www.biospectrumindia.com/views/81/27777/harnessing-ai-for-drug-discovery-positioning-indian-pharma-as-global-innovation-powerhouse.html | low | India | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-021] Zweidler (ex-Merck) says industry wastes about 7 billion USD a year repeating experiments others already did, but his proposal is a public-private partnership with shared molecules free of IP and data public after six months, with funding undescribed. Quantifies the duplication pain (opinion, 2011) yet points to free pooling, not paid sale. | https://mackinstitute.wharton.upenn.edu/2011/daniel-zweidler-on-the-benefits-of-sharing-failures/ | low | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-022] India: AIKosh (IndiaAI Datasets Platform) launched March 2025 with about 316 datasets for non-personal, anonymised data; company and startup contributions are voluntary and one report says datasets are not available for monetisation. Public free-commons signal that competes with paid sale and shows no paid private-seller market yet (secondary press; EOI PDF was an image and unreadable). | https://india.entrepreneur.com/news-and-trends/government-launches-ai-kosha-to-strengthen-ai-development/488155 | low | India | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-023] AGAINST (agent buyers): an agent-payment vendor article lists five competing agent payment protocols (x402, Stripe ACP/MPP, Google AP2, Visa TAP) and says agent purchasing is still early; it gives no transaction counts, and a fetch check found no spending figures and clear vendor bias. No independent evidence yet that enterprises let agents buy private data. | https://atxp.ai/blog/agent-economy-2026 | low | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-024] Pharma AI data deals are priced as upfront plus milestones, royalties, annual licence fees and equity (for example Recursion-Tempus 22M USD initial plus 22-42M a year; Incyte-Genesis 120M upfront in 2026 expansion); per-query pricing is not mentioned and negative or failed data is not mentioned as a licensed asset. Large buyers pay, but for curated platform-scale data and models, not query-level answers (consultancy source, amounts not independently confirmed). | https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/pharma-ai-data-licensing-deal-structures | low | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-025] Same source lists seller-side restrictions in pharma data licences: limited-use terms, bans on re-sharing or combining data, privacy consent needs, fear of strengthening competitors including big tech, and ownership of derived IP as a central negotiation point. Supports IP fear and legal-review cost as blockers, and suggests derived-answer ownership clauses would be needed. | https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/pharma-ai-data-licensing-deal-structures | low | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-026] BioSpace reports data ownership in pharma-AI partnerships is a recurring sticking point, with Astellas legal lead describing data deals as hard to negotiate and where the data is generated now a signing decision; no pricing discussion. Evidence of high legal friction per deal. | https://www.biospace.com/business/ai-data-becomes-sticking-point-in-biopharma-dealmaking | med | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-027] AGAINST (free alternative and incumbent): Eli Lilly TuneLab gives selected biotechs free access to models trained on Lilly data in return for federated model updates from their own data, with no IP requirements; about a dozen startups joined and it is distributed via Revvity Signals. Shows leading pharma prefers data-for-access exchange over cash, which undercuts a pay-per-query market. | https://www.drugdiscoverynews.com/eli-lilly-s-new-ai-platform-could-boost-biotechs-productivity-with-a-catch-16643 | med | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-028] A 2025 Science Advances-linked paper says negative chemical datasets are often inaccessible or not machine-readable; its negatives came from synthetic generation and public high-throughput data, not purchase. Confirms scarcity (demand side) but again no transaction. | https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12164950/ | med | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [M-029] AGAINST (free alternative): Open Reaction Database accepts failed reactions and launched 2021 with support from Google, Relay Therapeutics and others; free open repositories exist for negative chemistry data (repository listing seen via search only, not fetched in full). | https://recherche.data.gouv.fr/en/repositories/open-reaction-database-ord | low | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
