# Red-team findings (attacks on the idea)

Format: - [ID] claim | source URL | confidence (high/med/low) | jurisdiction | retrieved YYYY-MM-DD | status: unreviewed

## Findings

- [R-001] Dinur-Nissim result: answering too many queries too accurately lets an attacker reconstruct the underlying data; privacy needs noise of order sqrt(n), which hurts answer accuracy (a pay-per-query product sells accuracy) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reconstruction_attack | med | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [R-002] Differencing attacks (near-identical queries compared to isolate one record) are practical; Diffix-style noise was itself attacked in practice, so per-query noise alone is not a proven defense | https://differentialprivacy.org/diffix-attack/ | med | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [R-003] Indirect prompt injection: once a malicious text lands in retrieval top-K nearly any LLM can be hijacked; one poisoned email made GPT-4o exfiltrate SSH keys over 80 percent of the time; tested defenses insufficient. Seller content returned to a buyer agent is an injection channel | https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity26/presentation/chang | med | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [R-004] LLMs leak training data beyond verbatim memorization, so if the gateway uses a fine-tuned or LLM answerer, paraphrased answers can still leak partial seller content (theoretical for a RAG gateway, no direct paper found) | https://petsymposium.org/popets/2026/popets-2026-0139.php | low | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [R-005] Data marketplace cold start: owners of data valuable only in combination ask prices buyers will not meet; data is non-excludable so resale undercuts the seller; buyers see only a description and price before buying | https://magis.substack.com/p/how-data-markets-fail | low | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [R-006] Survey of 75 data marketplaces: buyers decide on description, price and sometimes an outdated sample only, so unverifiable answer quality is a documented buyer-side trust gap | https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.08874 | med | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [R-007] Sharing R&D without reasonable measures can forfeit trade secret status; a Delhi High Court decision is cited for loss of confidentiality absent reasonable efforts. Sellers risk their secrecy claim if the gateway leaks even partly | https://www.ibanet.org/trade-secret-protection-research-development | med | India | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [R-008] DPDP Act: failure to maintain reasonable security safeguards carries penalties up to Rs 250 crore, per instance; if seller KB holds personal data, a leaking gateway creates fiduciary exposure for whoever is the data fiduciary or processor (role allocation unresolved) | https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf | med | India | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [R-009] Moffatt v Air Canada: tribunal held the company liable for its chatbot's wrong answer and rejected the separate-entity defense; by analogy a gateway operator cannot easily disclaim wrong answers (tribunal decision, Canada, not binding in India) | https://www.mccarthy.ca/en/insights/blogs/techlex/moffatt-v-air-canada-misrepresentation-ai-chatbot | med | other (Canada) | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [R-010] Second pass, history of failure: Microsoft retired Azure DataMarket (launched 2010, closed March 2017) citing weak demand; Kasabi closed 2012 as the market grew too slowly; Infochimps pivoted away from its marketplace; Streamr reframed after low enterprise interest. Horizontal data marketplaces have a multi-vendor record of failing on demand, not technology | https://adtmag.com/articles/2016/11/18/azure-datamarket-shutdown.aspx | med | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [R-011] Second pass, hub-and-spoke risk (US): in July 2026 the Third Circuit revived price-fixing claims against casino-hotels and their shared pricing vendor Cendyn, treating exchange of non-public data through a common algorithm as a plausible plus factor, while a Yardi court granted summary judgment for lack of a horizontal agreement among spokes. A gateway where competing sellers and buyers feed one hub is exposed to the same theory; outcome depends on proof of a rim (search summary, cases not read) | https://www.mcguirewoods.com/client-resources/alerts/2026/8/third-circuit-revives-antitrust-claims-against-casino-hotels-use-of-algorithmic-pricing-software-cornish-adebiyi-v-caesars-entertainment-inc/ | low | US | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [R-012] Second pass, hub-and-spoke risk (India): the 2023 Competition Act amendment treats a non-participating facilitator as party to a cartel if it knowingly facilitates it, though CCI and NCLAT earlier dismissed a hub-and-spoke claim against ride-hailing platforms; so a platform exchanging competitors' R&D or pricing signals carries facilitator exposure (commentary source, statute not read; relates to L-017, L-033) | https://comp.circ.in/?p=5043 | low | India | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [R-013] Second pass, free alternatives: free community MCP servers already give agents keyless access to ChEMBL (public REST API) and PubMed (NCBI E-utilities, 3 to 10 requests per second free), so a buyer agent has zero-cost substitutes for public chemistry and literature knowledge; paid answers must be demonstrably non-public (community servers, code and uptime not verified) | https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/integrations/open-access | low | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [R-014] Second pass, procurement friction: no survey found measuring enterprises letting agents buy data autonomously; secondary guides cite Deloitte (21 percent mature agent governance, 73 percent name data privacy a top AI risk), Gartner (over 40 percent of agentic AI projects cancelled by end 2027) and buyers wanting a human before financial commitment, with real deployments taking 5 to 9 months. Vendor and aggregator figures, unverified at origin; supports a human-approved enterprise contract, not accountless agent purchase (extends M-023) | https://resources.rework.com/tools/ai-agents/best-enterprise-ai-agent-platforms-2026 | low | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [R-015] Second pass, EU angle: under the EU Data Act trade-secret data may be withheld unless confidentiality measures can be agreed, and refusal on the strongest grounds needs proof of a high likelihood of serious economic damage; the Act's sharing duties concern connected-product data and mandated B2B sharing, not voluntary sale, so it mainly shows regulators push toward more data access and weakens a scarcity premium for IoT-type data (law-firm summary, applicability date not confirmed by source) | https://www.williamfry.com/knowledge/data-act-deep-dive-part-2-data-and-use/ | low | EU | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [R-016] Second pass, TEE attack on the confidential-computing defense (builds on T-021, T-022, reviewed partly supported): the sub-1000 USD interposer forges attestation quotes that verify as current, so an attestation-based trust claim ("buyer cannot see raw data even if gateway host is a cloud") is falsifiable by anyone with brief physical access; a hosted multi-tenant gateway cannot rest its security promise on TEEs, and Intel and AMD call such attacks out of scope so no vendor fix is coming (primary page, mitigation is physical control) | https://tee.fail | high | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
- [R-017] Second pass, no evidence of payment for failed data (reviewed M-024 partly supported, M-028 partly supported): the pharma deal evidence is platform-scale licences (tens of millions) and the negative-chemistry evidence shows researchers building negatives synthetically or from public screens rather than buying them; two searches in this pass (Azure DataMarket history, free MCP data servers) also found no paid query-level knowledge market. Absence of evidence, but demand for failed data is unproven as paying demand (draws on M-024, M-028, M-013) | https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12164950/ | low | n/a | retrieved 2026-10-26 | status: unreviewed
