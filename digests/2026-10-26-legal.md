# Legal digest 2026-10-26

## New findings
- L-001: India has no trade-secret statute; protection rests on contract and breach of confidence.
- L-002: Breach of confidence needs an obligation of confidence, so buyers need express confidentiality and use limits.
- L-003: Contract Act s.27 may undercut broad buyer-use restrictions (Calcutta HC).
- L-004: EU Trade Secrets Directive requires "reasonable steps"; independent derivation is lawful acquisition.
- L-005: OBSTACLE. Aggregated answers could erode secrecy or make buyer derivation lawful (inference).
- L-006: Ownership of unused experiment data is unsettled (gap).
- L-007: Licence vs disclosure vs service is unresolved; limited-use licence framing suggested (inference).
- L-008: DPDP Rules notified Nov 2025; core duties commence ~13 May 2027.
- L-009: DPDP s.16 cross-border rule not yet in force; negative-list model; sectoral localisation still applies.
- L-010: DPDP penalties up to Rs 250 crore.
- L-011: DPDP has no B2B exemption.
- L-012: GDPR: pseudonymised data stays personal.
- L-013: OBSTACLE. "No raw data" does not remove DPDP/GDPR exposure (inference).
- L-014: Gap: US DTSA unresearched; primary texts unfetchable.

## Review verdicts
3 supported (L-001, L-008, L-009), 3 partly supported (L-002, L-003, L-010), 3 unsupported (L-006, L-007, L-011), 5 unverifiable (L-004, L-005, L-012, L-013, L-014). Most sources were secondary; indiacode, MeitY, EUR-Lex and verasafe were blocked.

## Open questions
Ticked on thin evidence (one supported finding each): ownership/trade secret (L-001 covers trade-secret basis only, not ownership), DPDP/GDPR (L-008 covers timing only), cross-border (L-009). Still open: licence vs disclosure vs service, liability, contract structure, competition law, intermediary vs broker. Follow-ups: US DTSA, primary DPDP/GDPR texts, chain of title for third-party data.

## Could make the idea unviable
- L-005: query aggregation may destroy the seller's trade-secret protection, which is the core asset.
- L-013: answers can still carry personal data, so output filtering and a lawful basis are mandatory.
- Both are unverified inference. No kill criteria exist yet; candidates for red-team.
